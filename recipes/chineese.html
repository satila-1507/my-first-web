Chineese Fried Rice


Chinese fried rice is a classic stir-fried dish made with cooked rice, aromatics, vegetables, eggs, and proteins tossed together over high heat in a wok.


Fried rice is believed to have started as a way to accommodate leftovers. Traditionally, Southern Chinese prefer their rice polished and plain, as a base staple to eat with meat and vegetables.[5] The vegetables, meat and rice leftovers from the day before—which have passed their prime but are still good to consume, and too good to be fed to animals—are seasoned with soy sauce, lard and garlic, and stir-fried, making a hot meal.


Ingredients

  • cooled, day-old white rice (long-grain or Jasmine work best because cold, dry grains prevent clumping).
  • Aromatics: Minced garlic, ginger, and the whites of green onions (scallions).
  • Protein: 2 eggs (scrambled into the rice), plus optional additions like diced chicken, shrimp, Chinese sausage (lop cheung), or tofu.
  • Diced carrots, green peas, and sweet corn.
  • Sauces & Seasonings
  • Cooking Fat: Neutral oil
  • Garnish: The green tops of chopped scallions (green onions).

"Steps"

  1. Prep the Aromatics and Veggies
  2. Scramble the Eggs
  3. Stir-Fry Aromatics and Fillings
  4. Break up the Rice
  5. Season and Sauce
  6. Finish and Garnish
