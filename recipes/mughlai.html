Mughlai Chicken

This is a rich, mildly spiced, and deeply aromatic Mughlai chicken curry that features tender chicken pieces enveloped in a luxurious, velvety gravy.

• Velvety and Creamy: The combination of pureed cashews, blanched almonds, and heavy cream gives the gravy a thick, silky texture that coats the back of a spoon. • Slightly Coarse Depth: The base of golden fried onions adds a subtle, pleasant texture and body to the sauce, ensuring it isn't just smooth but has a satisfying rusticity.

Ingredients
  • Chicken: 500g bone-in chicken pieces (cut into medium curry cuts)
  • The Marinade • Curd (Yogurt): ½ cup (whisked until smooth) • Ginger-Garlic Paste: 1 tablespoon • Lemon Juice: 1 tablespoon (freshly squeezed) • Red Chili Powder: 1 teaspoon (Kashmiri chili powder is best for a vibrant color without too much heat)
    • Turmeric Powder: ½ teaspoon
    • Salt: 1 teaspoon (or to taste)
  • The Secret Paste (Rich Base)
    • Onions: 2 medium onions (very thinly and evenly sliced for frying)
    • Cashews: 10–12 whole raw cashews (soaked in warm water for 15 minutes)
    • Almonds: 8–10 almonds (soaked in warm water, then skinned/peeled)
    • Green Chilies: 2–3 slit chilies (adjust based on how much kick you want)
  • Whole Spices (For Tempering)
    • Ghee / Oil: 3 tablespoons (ghee is highly recommended for that authentic royal flavor)
    • Cumin Seeds (Jeera): 1 teaspoon
    • Bay Leaves (Tejpatta): 2 small leaves
    • Green Cardamom (Elaichi): 4 pods
    • Black Cardamom: 1 pod
    • Cloves (Laung): 4–5 pieces
    • Cinnamon Stick (Dalchini): 1-inch piece
  • Ground Spices & Finishing Touches
    • Coriander Powder (Dhania): 1 tablespoon
    • Garam Masala Powder: 1 teaspoon
    • Heavy Cream: ¼ cup (fresh cream)
    • Dried Fenugreek Leaves (Kasuri Methi): 1 tablespoon
    • Water: ¾ cup (for the gravy)
    • Garnishes: Fresh coriander leaves (cilantro) and a few toasted almond flakes

Steps
  1. Marinate: Mix chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon, red chili, turmeric, and salt. Rest for 30 minutes
  2. Blend Paste: Fry sliced onions until golden. Blend them with soaked cashews, almonds, green chilies, and a splash of water into a smooth paste.
  3. Sear Chicken: Heat ghee. Fry whole spices (bay leaf, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, cumin) for 30 seconds, then sear the chicken for 5 minutes.
  4. Simmer Gravy: Stir in the onion-nut paste, coriander powder, and garam masala. Add ¾ cup water, cover, and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes.
  5. Finish: Stir in heavy cream and crushed kasuri methi. Simmer for 2 minutes, garnish with cilantro, and serve.
