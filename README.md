    <h1 class="header">Mughlai Chicken</h1><div class="image">
<img src="https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcQxAIhxUq0kjS6eCu9ysIEjgMjwiqeXbKMPZCTks5P77w&s=10" alt="Mughlai">
</div><div class="para">
<p><h2>Mughlai Chicken</h2>
This is a rich, mildly spiced, and deeply aromatic Mughlai chicken curry that features tender chicken pieces enveloped in a luxurious, velvety gravy.</p>
<p>• Velvety and Creamy: The combination of pureed cashews, blanched almonds, and heavy cream gives the gravy a thick, silky texture that coats the back of a spoon.
• Slightly Coarse Depth: The base of golden fried onions adds a subtle, pleasant texture and body to the sauce, ensuring it isn't just smooth but has a satisfying rusticity.
</p></div><div class="para">
<strong>Ingredients</strong><ul><li>Chicken: 500g bone-in chicken pieces (cut into medium curry cuts)</li>
<li>
The Marinade

• Curd (Yogurt): ½ cup (whisked until smooth)
• Ginger-Garlic Paste: 1 tablespoon
• Lemon Juice: 1 tablespoon (freshly squeezed)
• Red Chili Powder: 1 teaspoon (Kashmiri chili powder is best for a vibrant color without too much heat)<br>
• Turmeric Powder: ½ teaspoon<br>
• Salt: 1 teaspoon (or to taste)</li><li>
The Secret Paste (Rich Base)<br>

• Onions: 2 medium onions (very thinly and evenly sliced for frying)<br>
• Cashews: 10–12 whole raw cashews (soaked in warm water for 15 minutes)<br>
• Almonds: 8–10 almonds (soaked in warm water, then skinned/peeled)<br>
• Green Chilies: 2–3 slit chilies (adjust based on how much kick you want)</li>
<li>
Whole Spices (For Tempering)<br>

• Ghee / Oil: 3 tablespoons (ghee is highly recommended for that authentic royal flavor)<br>
• Cumin Seeds (Jeera): 1 teaspoon<br>
• Bay Leaves (Tejpatta): 2 small leaves<br>
• Green Cardamom (Elaichi): 4 pods<br>
• Black Cardamom: 1 pod<br>
• Cloves (Laung): 4–5 pieces<br>
• Cinnamon Stick (Dalchini): 1-inch piece</li>
<li>
Ground Spices & Finishing Touches<br>

• Coriander Powder (Dhania): 1 tablespoon<br>
• Garam Masala Powder: 1 teaspoon<br>
• Heavy Cream: ¼ cup (fresh cream)<br>
• Dried Fenugreek Leaves (Kasuri Methi): 1 tablespoon<br>
• Water: ¾ cup (for the gravy)<br>
• Garnishes: Fresh coriander leaves (cilantro) and a few toasted almond flakes</li></ul><br>
</div><div class="para">
<strong>Steps</strong><ol><li>Marinate: Mix chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon, red chili, turmeric, and salt. Rest for 30 minutes</li>
<li>Blend Paste: Fry sliced onions until golden. Blend them with soaked cashews, almonds, green chilies, and a splash of water into a smooth paste.</li>
<li>Sear Chicken: Heat ghee. Fry whole spices (bay leaf, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, cumin) for 30 seconds, then sear the chicken for 5 minutes.</li>
<li>Simmer Gravy: Stir in the onion-nut paste, coriander powder, and garam masala. Add ¾ cup water, cover, and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes.</li>
<li>Finish: Stir in heavy cream and crushed kasuri methi. Simmer for 2 minutes, garnish with cilantro, and serve.</li></ol>
</div>
